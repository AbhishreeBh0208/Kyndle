## 🌍 Vision

Gaming has become one of the world's largest communities.

Millions of players invest thousands of hours into achievements, progression, and teamwork.

Kyndle exists to transform that effort into something that extends beyond the screen.

Our mission is to build a platform where gaming achievements contribute toward meaningful real-world impact while giving players a single place to showcase their gaming identity.
